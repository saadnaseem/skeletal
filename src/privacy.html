Privacy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

  • You can use Lone Pair without an account. Signing in with Google is optional.
  • Your drawings and projects stay on your computer (in your browser and in files you save). They are never uploaded to our server, unless you choose to attach one to a feedback message.
  • If you sign in, we keep your name, email address and when you visit, so we know who uses Lone Pair and can tell you about important changes.

What we keep if you sign in with Google

DataWhy
Your Google account ID, name and email addressTo recognise you when you come back, and to contact you about the service
When you first signed in, when you last visited, and how many times you have signed inTo understand how Lone Pair is used
A sign-in cookie (lp_session), valid for 30 daysTo keep you signed in. It is strictly necessary for signing in and is not used for advertising

We ask Google only for your basic profile (name and email). We do not get your password, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not send you marketing emails unless you have agreed to receive them.
  • We do not upload, read or store your drawings (except a drawing you attach to feedback yourself).

Feedback messages

When you use Feedback, your message is emailed to the person who runs Lone Pair, together with the email address you give (optional, so we can reply), your browser name and version, and, only if you tick the box, your current drawing as SMILES. The message is not stored on our server; it lives in that email inbox. Ask us to delete it at any time.

To stop floods of messages, the server briefly remembers how many messages came from each address (IPv6 addresses by their /64 block) in its memory. This is never written to disk or sent on, and it is forgotten after 10 minutes.

Features that contact other services

The hosted site loads its interface font (Schibsted Grotesk) from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for it, which shows them your IP address and browser. Google says it does not use this to build profiles (Google Fonts privacy FAQ). Lone Pair works the same if the font is blocked.

A few optional tools send a structure to a public database, only when you click them: Look up name and CAS number and inserting a compound by name (PubChem, US National Library of Medicine), and Find in databases (PubChem, RCSB PDB, ChEMBL, Google). They send only the structure's SMILES or InChIKey, never your account details.

Visitor statistics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies or track you across sites.

Where your data is kept

Lone Pair runs on Cloudflare. Account data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. We keep it until you delete your account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: sign in, click your name in the top bar, then Delete my account. Your name, email and visit records are removed straight away.
  • Sign out at any time from the same menu.
  • You can also revoke Lone Pair's access in your Google account settings under "Third-party apps and services".

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@lonepair.app.
