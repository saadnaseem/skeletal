Lone Pair: a free chemical structure editor

Lone Pair draws molecules, reactions and arrow-pushing mechanisms for papers, theses, posters and slides. It runs entirely in your browser: no install, no account, and your drawings stay on your computer. You can also save it as a single file that works offline.

Start drawing

Drawing

  • Single, double, triple, wedge, hashed, wavy and bold bonds; rings; chains with a live atom count.
  • Any element, common abbreviations (OMe, Boc, Ph, Ts and more), charges, lone pairs and radicals.
  • Reaction, equilibrium, retrosynthetic and resonance arrows, and curved electron-pushing arrows (full and fishhook).
  • Insert structures from SMILES, InChI, molfiles, or a compound name or CAS number.
  • Clean up, align, group, smart guides, TLC plates and enhanced stereochemistry labels.

Publication-quality figures

  • Journal styles: ACS Document 1996, RSC, Nature Reviews and slides, with ChemDraw-style drawing settings.
  • Copy a transparent high-resolution image into PowerPoint or Word, or save PNG (600 dpi), SVG, PDF or EPS.
  • Open and save ChemDraw files (CDXML and CDX), plus molfile, SD file, RXN, CML, Marvin, SMILES, InChI and chemfig for LaTeX.

Chemistry tools

  • ¹H and ¹³C NMR prediction, an NMR solving guide, and NMR solvent and impurity peaks.
  • Mass spectrum with isotope patterns, formula, exact mass, elemental analysis, cLogP, LogS and tPSA.
  • pKa and bond dissociation energy estimates, a 3D conformer viewer, and name and CAS number lookup on PubChem.

Who it's for

Students, teachers and researchers who need clean chemical structures without a ChemDraw licence, and anyone who wants to open or share ChemDraw files from any computer.
